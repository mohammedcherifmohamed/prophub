About the Property

Villa Mio is a newly constructed architectural masterpiece for sale in El Madroñal, Benahavis. Designed by acclaimed architect Antonio Morillo, this luxury villa is set to complete in 2025 and redefines high-end hillside living on the Costa del Sol.

With striking design, elevated positioning, and panoramic sea and mountain views, the property offers an exclusive lifestyle in one of southern Spain’s most secure and prestigious gated communities.

Spanning 1,520 square metres across three levels with 492 square metres of terraces, the villa seamlessly blends contemporary sophistication with Mediterranean charm. A long private driveway leads to generous entertaining spaces, landscaped gardens, and exceptional indoor-outdoor living.
