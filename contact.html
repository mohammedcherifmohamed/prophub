Knowledge & Compliance

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreign nationals purchase freehold property in Dubai? ⌄

Yes. Dubai designates prominent master developments (including Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, and Dubai Creek Harbour) as freehold zones, allowing 100% foreign freehold ownership and eligibility for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa for qualifying investments.

How are investor funds protected during off-plan construction? ⌄

Under Dubai Land Department (DLD) Law No. 8 of 2007, every developer must establish an official Escrow Account with an accredited bank. Buyer installments are deposited directly into escrow and disbursed solely upon third-party verified construction milestone inspections.

How does the private viewing process work for international clients? ⌄

For overseas investors, we coordinate high-definition live virtual tours with our licensed advisors, 3D architectural renders, drone aerial site overviews, and comprehensive legal review before executing any commitment.
